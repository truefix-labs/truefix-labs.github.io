ABOUT TRUEFIX STUDIO

One app for research, testing,
and checking trades.

TrueFix Studio connects the brokers, exchanges, market-data, and AI services you choose. We are building it so people can do more of their market work in one place without moving their money to TrueFix.

TrueFix is software. It does not open financial accounts, hold money, recommend investments, or promise returns.

See how the product works ↗

01 / PURPOSE

Why we are
building TrueFix.

01

Spend less time switching tools

Prices, news, strategy tests, accounts, and risk checks often live in separate apps. TrueFix brings them together and still shows where each piece of information came from.

02

Choose how far you want to go

Use it only for research, keep a connection read-only, test with simulated money, or review a real trading action. You choose each step.

03

Keep AI and strategies under control

The AI assistant and automated strategies can use only the data, tools, accounts, and limits you allow.

03 / PROJECT BOUNDARIES

What stays
under your control.

Main settings stay on your device

The current product does not require a TrueFix cloud account. You choose and configure every outside service.

Money stays with your broker or exchange

TrueFix does not accept deposits or take custody of user funds.

Review remains your responsibility

AI and automation do not replace account permissions, risk checks, or your decision to approve an action.

TrueFix Studio is still before its first stable release. Read the guide, start read-only or simulated, and tell us what remains unclear.
