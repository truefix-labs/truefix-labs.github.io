WHAT TRUEFIX ADDS

Keep your platforms.
Add the missing workflow.

TrueFix does not replace your broker or hold your money. It adds one workspace for the research, testing, and review that normally happens across disconnected tools.

01

Research across sources

Compare market data, news, account context, and the source behind a signal without losing track of which service supplied it.

02

Use AI and Quant as tools

Ask an Agent to organize information or replay a strategy, while keeping its tools, budget, data, and permissions limited.

03

Review before you act

Check the exact Provider, account, environment, instrument, fees, and risk before an approved instruction is sent.

Your accounts stay where they are.TrueFix does not accept deposits. Connections are configured by you, and every available action still depends on the permissions of the selected Provider.

04 / SAFETY

Control stays
with you.

TrueFix does not accept deposits or create a TrueFix cloud account. You configure each connection and decide which permissions, environment, and account can be used.

01

Connections use explicit permissions

A data or trading feature is available only when the selected Provider, account, market, environment, and permission support it.

Provider → Account → Permission
02

Review before money moves

Before submission, confirm the exact account, instrument, order, fees, and risk. An uncertain result is checked instead of automatically sent again.

Review → Risk check → Submit
03

AI assists within limits

An Agent can analyze with the tools you allow. Trading permission is separate, and an AI suggestion is not treated as an approved order.

Analyze → Suggest → Human review

07 / ABOUT US

About TrueFix Studio.
Why we are building it.

TrueFix Studio is an independent, pre-release software project for people who already use brokers, exchanges, market data, or systematic strategies.

The goal is practical: reduce tool switching, make the source of information visible, and keep automation inside limits the user can inspect.

LOCAL-FIRST

Your profile starts on this device

TrueFix does not require a TrueFix cloud account. Connections to outside services are configured by you for the features you choose to use.

NO CUSTODY

Your funds stay with your Provider

TrueFix is software, not a broker, exchange, bank, or investment adviser. It does not accept deposits or promise returns.

USER CONTROL

You choose how far the workflow goes

Use research only, stay read-only, test in simulation, or configure an authorized execution path. AI assistance does not remove your responsibility to review.
