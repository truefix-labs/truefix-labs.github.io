ABOUT TRUEFIX STUDIO

A practical layer
above the platforms you use.

TrueFix Studio is an independent, pre-release software project. We are building it to reduce tool switching, keep information sources visible, and make automated actions easier to inspect and control.

TrueFix is software—not a broker, exchange, bank, custodian, or investment adviser.

See how the product works ↗

01 / PURPOSE

Why we are
building TrueFix.

01

Less fragmented context

Market data, research, strategies, accounts, and risk checks often live in separate tools. TrueFix brings the workflow together while keeping the original Provider visible.

02

More control before action

Users can stop at research, stay read-only, test in simulation, or configure a reviewed execution path. Every step should be explicit.

03

AI and Quant as tools

Agents and strategies should operate with clear data, tool, budget, account, and risk limits—not open-ended authority.

03 / PROJECT BOUNDARIES

What stays
under your control.

Local-first profile

The current product does not require a TrueFix cloud account. You configure the outside services you want to use.

Funds stay with the Provider

TrueFix does not accept deposits or take custody of user funds.

Review remains your responsibility

AI assistance and automation do not replace account permissions, risk checks, or the user’s responsibility to review an action.

TrueFix Studio is still before its first stable release. Read the guide, start read-only or simulated, and tell us what remains unclear.
