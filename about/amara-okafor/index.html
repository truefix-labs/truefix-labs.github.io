PRODUCT DESIGN PERSPECTIVE

Amara Okafor

Systems Architecture

The question this perspective asks

Can one connected service fail without taking down the rest of the app?

This perspective separates providers and responsibilities. A broken connector should be isolated, its permissions should stay narrow, and the rest of the workspace should remain useful.

What it checks

IsolationOne provider failure stays inside its boundary.
Narrow accessEach connector gets only the access it needs.
Predictable recoveryRestarting one part does not reset everything.
