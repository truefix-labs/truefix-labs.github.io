PRODUCT DESIGN PERSPECTIVE

Park Min-seo

Runtime Reliability

The question this perspective asks

When a connection fails, what will the user see and what happens next?

Connected services fail, slow down, and reconnect. This perspective asks TrueFix to show the real state, avoid silent retries that change outcomes, and recover without duplicating an action.

What it checks

Visible statusConnected, delayed, retrying, and failed mean different things.
Bounded retryRetries stop and remain visible.
Safe recoveryRecovery avoids duplicate orders or hidden state changes.
