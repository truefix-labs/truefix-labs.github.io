AI STRATEGY CREATION & DEPLOYMENT

Tell AI your trading rules.
Generate, test, then deploy.

Describe your goal to the Agent in Desktop Strategy Engineering. Check the strategy and test results, then review the proposal in Deployment, approve it, and activate it.

An example request to AI

Draft a moving-average crossover strategy. Specify the data, entry and exit conditions, and risk limits. Validate it with historical data and ask me to review it before deployment.
  1. 01

    Draft with AI

    Specify instruments, rules, and constraints. Answer any clarification questions.

  2. 02

    Validate and test

    Inspect the strategy revision and use backtesting or replay to check behavior and risk.

  3. 03

    Review the proposal

    Check the revision, data sources, account, environment, and risk limits.

  4. 04

    Approve and activate

    Approve the deployment on Desktop, then explicitly activate the approved proposal.

  5. 05

    Monitor and pause

    Inspect runtime status, logs, and decisions. Pause or stop when needed.

Generating a strategy does not start live execution. Deployment creation and Live arming are Desktop-only; H5 supports monitoring, per-decision approval, pause, and stop. Execution remains subject to account permissions and risk checks.

04 / SAFETY

Control stays
with you.

TrueFix does not accept deposits or open a financial account for you. You choose every connected service, account, permission, and test or live mode.

01

You choose what each connection can do

A connection can only use the market data or trading permissions allowed by the broker, exchange, or data service you selected.

Service → Account → Permission
02

Check before sending a trade

Before sending, confirm the account, product, direction, amount, price, fees, and risk. If the result is unclear, TrueFix checks the existing order instead of sending it again.

Check → Risk check → Send
03

The AI assistant has limits

The AI assistant can use only the tools you allow. Giving it access to research does not give it permission to trade, and a suggestion is never an approved order.

Analyze → Suggest → You review

07 / ABOUT US

About TrueFix Studio.
Why we are building it.

TrueFix Studio is an independent, pre-release desktop app for people who already use brokers, exchanges, market data, or rule-based trading strategies.

We are building it to reduce switching between tools, make information sources easy to check, and keep AI and automation under the user's control.

LOCAL-FIRST

Your profile starts on this device

TrueFix does not require a TrueFix cloud account. Connections to outside services are configured by you for the features you choose to use.

NO CUSTODY

Your money stays with your broker or exchange

TrueFix is software, not a broker, exchange, bank, or investment adviser. It does not accept deposits, hold money, or promise returns.

USER CONTROL

You choose how far the workflow goes

Use research only, stay read-only, test in simulation, or configure an authorized execution path. AI assistance does not remove your responsibility to review.
