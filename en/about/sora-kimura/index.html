PRODUCT DESIGN PERSPECTIVE

Sora Kimura

Human Factors & Risk

The question this perspective asks

Are permission, uncertainty, and risk clear before a person acts?

This perspective checks what the interface communicates at the moment of decision. Users should see what is known, what is uncertain, which account is affected, and whether an action can be undone.

What it checks

Plain choicesControls describe consequences, not internal jargon.
Risk before actionAccount, size, cost, and risk appear before approval.
Clear statusA proposal is never mistaken for a completed trade.
