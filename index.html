01 / PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Understand the market.
Test the decision. Control the action.

TrueFix Studio is a multi-provider trading workspace that keeps research, evidence, strategy, risk review, and execution in one traceable flow.

01

WHAT IT IS

One evidence-backed decision chain

Instead of separating charts, research, strategies, accounts, and orders across unrelated tools, TrueFix keeps their identity, source, and state connected.

02

WHO IT IS FOR

Traders, quant builders, and research or risk teams

Current access is limited to invited private-beta testers. Every usable capability still depends on the selected provider, account, environment, and entitlement.

03

WHAT THEY DO

Observe → verify → test → review → execute

A user can inspect a signal and its sources, replay a strategy, choose an authorized account, review fees and risk, then submit and reconcile the result.

04 / PRINCIPLES

Trading infrastructure
should never guess.

TrueFix draws deliberate boundaries: when mappings, accounts, permissions, or freshness are missing, it stops instead of quietly choosing a plausible answer.

01

Capabilities resolve from evidence

ProviderDefinition, ClientInstance, market, product, environment, and entitlement jointly decide whether a capability exists now.

CapabilityScope → Evidence → Route
02

Execution never guesses

Orders bind to an exact instrument, provider, ClientInstance, account, and environment. Unknown outcomes can only be queried—not resubmitted.

Exact identity → RiskGuard → Execution
03

AI is authorized, not unleashed

Research and strategy output cannot place orders directly. Trading intent must carry versions, expiry, evidence, and account constraints.

Signal → Decision → Review → Order
